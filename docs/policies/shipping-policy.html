Shipping Policy

Last Updated: March 3, 2026

1. Overview

All physical products (canvas prints, posters, apparel, etc.) are printed and shipped by Gelato, our global print-on-demand fulfillment partner. Gelato operates a worldwide network of production facilities and ships to most countries.

Digital portrait files are delivered electronically — they are not subject to this Shipping Policy.

2. How Fulfillment Works

Once your order is placed and payment is confirmed, the process is as follows:

  1. Your AI-generated portrait is sent to Gelato's nearest production facility
  2. Gelato prints, quality-checks, and packages your product
  3. Your order is shipped by a local carrier to your delivery address
  4. Tracking information is automatically sent to you by email

3. Production Times

Production begins after payment is confirmed. Estimated production times by product type:

  • Canvas Prints: 2–5 business days
  • Posters: 1–3 business days
  • Apparel: 2–4 business days
  • Other products: Varies by type — indicated on the product page

These are estimates and may be longer during peak periods (holidays, high order volume).

4. Delivery Times

Delivery times are calculated from the date your order ships, after production is complete.

Domestic (United States):

  • Standard: 5–10 business days
  • Express: 2–5 business days (where available)

International:

  • Europe: 7–14 business days
  • Canada: 7–12 business days
  • Australia / New Zealand: 10–18 business days
  • Asia: 10–20 business days
  • Rest of world: 10–25 business days

Delivery times are estimates only and not guaranteed. Delays may occur due to customs, carrier issues, or events outside our control.

5. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on:

  • Product type, size, and weight
  • Delivery destination
  • Selected shipping method

The final shipping cost is displayed before you confirm your order. We do not add hidden fees.

6. Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you will receive an email containing:

  • Your tracking number
  • Carrier name
  • A link to track your shipment
  • Estimated delivery date

Tracking information is typically available within 24–48 hours of shipment. You can also view your order status in your account area on our website.

7. International Shipping

Customs Duties and Import Taxes

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, or VAT upon arrival in your country. These charges are the sole responsibility of the customer. We are not responsible for any delays caused by customs processing.

Restricted Destinations

Some products may not be available for delivery to certain countries due to local regulations or carrier limitations. This will be indicated at checkout if your destination is affected.

8. Shipping Address

Please ensure your shipping address is complete and accurate before placing your order. We are not responsible for orders shipped to incorrect addresses provided at checkout.

If you need to change your shipping address after placing an order, contact us immediately at hello@petportraitai.com. Address changes cannot be guaranteed once an order has entered production.

9. Lost or Missing Packages

If your package has not arrived within the estimated delivery window:

  1. Check the tracking information provided in your shipping confirmation email
  2. Contact the carrier directly with your tracking number
  3. If the carrier cannot locate your package, contact us within 30 days of the estimated delivery date, including your order number and tracking information

We will work with Gelato and the carrier to investigate and arrange a replacement or refund where appropriate.

10. Damaged Packages

If your order arrives damaged:

  1. Contact us within 7 days of delivery
  2. Include your order number and photos of the damaged item and packaging
  3. Do not discard the damaged item until we advise you to do so

We will work with Gelato to arrange a replacement or refund.

11. Multiple Items — Separate Shipments

If your order contains multiple items, they may ship from different Gelato production facilities. You will receive a separate tracking number for each shipment. No additional shipping fees will be charged for split shipments.

12. Peak Seasons and Delays

During busy periods — including Black Friday, Christmas, and other holidays — both production and delivery times may be longer than usual. We recommend placing orders well in advance to receive your portrait on time.

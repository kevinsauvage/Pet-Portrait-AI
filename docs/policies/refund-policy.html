Return and Refund Policy

Last Updated: March 3, 2026

1. Overview

We want you to love your pet portrait. Because each portrait is uniquely generated using AI and your pet's photo, all products are made-to-order and personalised. Please read this policy carefully before placing your order.

2. Digital Downloads

Digital portrait files are delivered electronically immediately after purchase. Digital downloads are non-refundable once delivered, as the file has been generated specifically for you and can be used immediately upon delivery.

Exceptions may be made at our discretion if:

  • The file was corrupted or unreadable at the time of delivery
  • There was a technical error on our part that prevented correct delivery

If you experience a technical issue, please contact us within 7 days of purchase at hello@petportraitai.com.

3. Physical Products (Canvas, Posters, Apparel)

All physical products are custom-printed by our fulfillment partner Gelato and made to order. As such, we do not accept returns or exchanges for:

  • Change of mind
  • Choosing the wrong size or product type
  • Dissatisfaction with the AI art style chosen at the time of order

3.1 Eligible Refund or Replacement Situations

We will offer a full refund or free replacement in the following cases:

  • Damaged in transit: Your product arrives visibly damaged due to shipping
  • Manufacturing defect: The product has a print defect, misalignment, or quality issue caused during production
  • Wrong item received: You received a product different from what you ordered (wrong size, wrong product type)
  • Lost in transit: Your package is confirmed lost by the carrier and does not arrive within 30 days of the estimated delivery date

3.2 How to Request a Refund or Replacement

To submit a claim, contact us within 30 days of the delivery date (or estimated delivery date for lost packages) at hello@petportraitai.com with:

  • Your order number
  • A description of the issue
  • Clear photos of the product and packaging (for damaged or defective items)

We will respond within 2 business days to assess your claim and confirm next steps.

4. Refund Process

Once a refund is approved:

  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method
  • Processing time: 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider
  • You will receive a confirmation email when the refund has been issued

We do not charge restocking fees.

5. Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before they enter production. Once Gelato has started processing your order, cancellation may not be possible.

To request a cancellation, contact us immediately after placing your order at hello@petportraitai.com. Include your order number and we will confirm whether cancellation is still possible.

If cancellation is successful, a full refund will be issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

6. EU Consumer Rights

If you are a consumer in the European Union, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of delivery for standard goods. However, under EU Directive 2011/83/EU, this right does not apply to goods that are clearly personalised or made to order — which includes all PetPortrait AI products (both digital and physical), as they are created specifically using your pet's photo.

For any dispute not resolved directly with us, EU consumers may use the EU Online Dispute Resolution platform.
